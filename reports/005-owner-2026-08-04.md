## C) Level and Strategy

**Not a stretch worth taking, specifically because it's a *founding* PM role.** The `fundamentals-plan.md` PM overlay already flags this dynamic generally — "expect PM screens to probe hard on why PM now" — and a founding/first-PM-on-a-product-line req raises that bar further, since there's no senior PM structure to backstop a first-timer's learning curve.

**If the PM archetype is still worth pursuing at Owner or elsewhere:** target a PM role **embedded in an established team with existing PM peers and process**, not a "founding" or "0-to-1 sole owner" PM req. That is a meaningfully easier door to walk through, and it's worth re-scanning for that shape specifically rather than treating every PM title as equivalent.
