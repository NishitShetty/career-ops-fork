## C) Level and Strategy

**1. Level detected vs natural level**

The title carries no band ("Forward Deployed Engineer", not Senior/Staff), which at a company of Cohere's stage usually means the band is set at offer rather than at posting — consistent with the 210K-wide advertised range (see Block G, signal 13). Responsibilities are Senior IC: own deployments end to end, face customers unaccompanied, work across an unfamiliar codebase. The candidate's natural level for the FDE archetype is **Senior IC / Staff-adjacent**: 11+ years, Single-Threaded Ownership of a named capability across 6 Epics, architectural trade-off ownership (Vector Tiles vs. GeoJSON POC; Keycloak stalemate). The gap is domain, not seniority.

**2. Sell senior without lying**

Lead with the two things most FDE applicants cannot show:

- **"I have deployed into someone else's environment, on their schedule, with their executives watching."** SAT and system deployment for Inmarsat in Michigan and Perth; the aviation traffic-drop debug resolved before the flight landed; the 4:00 AM terminal-movement fix ahead of a 10:00 AM live executive demo. Concrete, dated, and customer-named.
- **"I own the part that comes after the demo."** Helm standardization cutting build/deploy from 1 hour to 5 minutes; memory/CPU leak resolution in map-converter services through profiling; 100% system stability during high-scale RVT. Forward-deployed work fails at rollout and operability, not at prototype.

Then name the gap **before they ask it** — an unprompted, accurate self-assessment reads senior; a discovered gap reads as overclaiming. Suggested phrasing: *"I have not shipped RAG or agentic systems. What I bring is the deployment and customer surface of forward-deployed work, from a domain where the customer is a satellite operator instead of a department. I would expect the model layer to be my learning curve and the deployment layer to be where I contribute from week one."*

There is no founder experience in the record — do not manufacture one.

**3. If they downlevel me**

Downlevelling is a live possibility given the GenAI gap. Guidance:

- The **band, not the title, is the decision variable.** `config/profile.yml` sets `minimum: "CAD 200,000"` against a `current: "CAD 175,000"`. Any offer at or below CAD 200,000 base fails the stated floor regardless of level — see Block D.
- If the title is junior to expectation but base clears the floor, accept the title and negotiate a **6-month review with written criteria**, anchored on the one thing that will be objectively true by then: independently delivered customer deployments.
- Do not trade base for equity at a private company on a first offer. Cohere's last publicly reported valuation is roughly $7B (Sept 2025 extension), with a Series E led by Schwarz Group reported in April 2026 — a mark, not liquidity.
- **Management track:** none visible in the JD. Per `modes/_profile.md`, a strong, high-comp Canadian role at an employer with a US entity is **not** down-scored for being IC. Worth asking whether an internal IC-to-lead path exists; Cohere's own board carries "Forward Deployed Engineering, Tech Lead" and "Engineering Manager, FDE Agentic Platform" postings, so the ladder above this seat demonstrably exists.
