### The domain question, answered honestly

The tempting framing is "Ciena is a telecom company, the candidate has 11 years of telecom, and Ciena's Blue Planet is network management — the candidate's exact domain." Two of those three are true and the third does not apply to this req.

**Blue Planet is not this posting.** This req sits in "the Modem and Components R&D organization" building WaveLogic. Blue Planet orchestration/NMS is a separate Ciena product line under a separate organization. The candidate's 11-year NMS domain has no bearing on this hiring manager's evaluation.

**Does SatCom modem experience transfer to coherent optical modems?** Only at the level of vocabulary, not at the level of engineering. The overlap and the gap:

- **What genuinely transfers:** the word "modem", general telecom systems literacy, digital signal processing concepts at a conceptual level, terminal-level verification instincts ("unblocking critical verification for IQ-series modems"), and the fact that both are physical-layer products with a firmware component. Working across a hardware/firmware boundary is real and is worth something.
- **What does not transfer:** the candidate's IQ-series modem exposure is as a **Systems Engineer in Test** verifying finished satellite terminals in the field and in the lab. Coherent optical modem development at Ciena is **custom DSP ASIC design** — a silicon program with tape-out gates, foundry process nodes, wafer availability, packaging, and a hardware NPI ramp. The JD names exactly this: "silicon tape-out dates, process development initiatives, and technology availability", "new product introduction through mass production lifecycle phases", "minimizing material exposure". None of that vocabulary has an analogue in a career spent on satellite ground terminals and cloud-native NMS software.

The realistic read: this is a **domain stretch**, and the specific thing being stretched — semiconductor program management — is the load-bearing part of the job, not a peripheral nice-to-have. A hiring manager staffing a WaveLogic program will have a shortlist of candidates who have shepherded silicon to tape-out before. Framing 11 SatCom years as "modem experience" here would be exactly the tool-of-trade conflation the source-of-truth rules forbid, and it would not survive a 20-minute screen with an optical R&D manager.
