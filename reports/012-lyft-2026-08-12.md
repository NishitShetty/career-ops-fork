- Portfolio coverage? None — `modes/_profile.md` records no portfolio.
- Mitigation: do not paper over this. If Lyft ever comes back at the right level, a small Airflow-orchestrated Kafka→Flink demo over open GTFS/trip data would convert a stated gap into a demonstrated one. Nothing may be claimed in application material today.

**2. C++ (absent from `cv.md`).**
- Blocker? **Soft.** The JD says "ideally in GoLang, C++, or Python" — any one of the three satisfies it on its face, and Go and Python are both on record.
- Mitigation: lead with Go; never imply C++. The firmware/bootloader work (U-Boot, IQ DT) is embedded systems credibility, but `cv.md` does not name the language, so no C or C++ claim may be made from it.

**3. Routing/graph algorithms (absent).**
- Blocker? **Soft for hiring, hard for honest self-assessment.** Lyft will screen on algorithmic depth for a routing team.
- Adjacent experience? Geospatial data modelling, tile-vs-payload latency trade-offs, and the ERD cardinality standard show the right kind of thinking about spatial data structures.
- Mitigation: the Vector Tiles vs. GeoJSON POC is the honest bridge — "I have optimized how spatial data moves and renders, not how paths are computed over it." Framing only; no claim beyond it.

**4. Consumer scale (millions of requests/day).**
- Blocker? **Soft.** Mission-critical enterprise SatCom scale is real but different in shape.
- Mitigation: emphasize reliability outcomes ("100% system stability during high-scale RVT") over raw QPS, which is not on record.
