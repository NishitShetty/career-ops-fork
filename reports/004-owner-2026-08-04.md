Companies in this position typically use one of three mechanisms, each with different consequences:
1. **A genuine Canadian subsidiary** — normal T4 employment, RRSP-type benefits possible, no issue.
2. **An Employer of Record (EOR)** (e.g., Deel, Remote.com, Papaya Global) — legally employed by the EOR, contracted out to Owner. Fully legitimate and common, but changes benefits structure, notice periods, and sometimes total comp (EOR fees are sometimes passed through as a lower net offer).
3. **Independent contractor status** — the candidate invoices Owner directly. This has materially different tax treatment (no CPP/EI employer contributions, no employment standards protections under Ontario law) and is worth knowing before any offer conversation, not after.

**This is not a legitimacy red flag** — nothing about the posting or the company suggests anything improper — but it is a **material unknown that changes the actual value of the USD 190-220K number**, and it should be asked about directly and early, ideally on the recruiter screen, before any further customization effort.
