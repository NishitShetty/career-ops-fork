### Honest framing of the domain match

This is the **most direct geospatial match in the candidate's pipeline** — the only req where `cv.md` contains the literal phrases the JD trades in (Vector Tiles, GeoJSON, map services, real-time asset tracking). That is worth stating plainly and it is worth using in outreach.

It is equally important not to overstate it. Three boundaries:

1. **Visualization ≠ map data.** The candidate owned how map data was *rendered and delivered* to an operator UI. This team owns where the map data *comes from*, how conflicting sources get reconciled, and whether the geometry is correct. Different problem, adjacent stack.

Lyft's map covers open-ended public road networks feeding 262M rides per quarter. `article-digest.md` flags this explicitly under "Gaps worth closing → Quantified scale": the record quantifies *change* (92%, 6 Epics) but rarely *scale*.
3. **The POC's result is undocumented.** `article-digest.md` marks the Vector Tiles vs. GeoJSON latency numbers as `[TO FILL]`. The single most reusable technical story for any mapping interview currently has no measured outcome attached to it. Fixing that is high-value regardless of which mapping req is pursued.
