**Base case:** Canadian citizen based in Ottawa, ON. Full work authorization in Canada — Canadian roles (including relocation within Canada) score **Not needed** on work authorization, no location penalty, regardless of remote/hybrid/on-site.

**US roles — the constraint that shapes scoring and targeting.** Citizenship and country of birth point in opposite directions. Canadian citizenship unlocks fast *temporary* entry (TN) but buys nothing on the permanent-residency side, which queues by country of birth. The candidate's birth-country queue is heavily backlogged in every employment category, so **"offers sponsorship" does not mean "solves permanent residency."** Score sponsorship language by *which route it implies*, never as a monolith.

**Pathway ranking (detail and reasoning are out of scope for this repo):**
1. **TN (USMCA)** — fastest entry. Qualifying titles are **"Engineer"** or **"Computer Systems Analyst"**; "Software Engineer"/"Software Developer" are *not* USMCA categories, and CBP assesses duties rather than titles. **TN is single-intent — a bridge only, never the status a green card is pursued under.**
2. **Cap-exempt H-1B** — under verification; may already be available.
3. **L-1A → EB-1C** — the active path. Requires an employer with both a Canadian and a US entity.
4. **O-1A → EB-1A** — self-petitioned, employer-independent, 2-3 year build.
5. **E-2** — available via Canadian citizenship; no permanent-residency path.
6. **EB-5 reserved set-aside** — capital-gated.

**HARD DQ — ITAR / "US person".** Roles requiring US citizenship, permanent residency, a security clearance, or "US person" status for ITAR/EAR-controlled work are a **hard blocker today** and score 1.0. TN, H-1B, L-1A, and dependent statuses are none of those. This disqualifies most US space and defense employers until permanent residency is obtained. Flag it explicitly rather than scoring the role on fit.

**Open constraint:** a prior US nonimmigrant visa application outcome is unresolved and may restrict border-adjudicated entry. **Do not recommend a TN port-of-entry attempt** until it is resolved offline.

**Work-authorization scoring.** Apply these at the work-authorization check in the evaluation mode (currently `modes/oferta.md` → Block A → *Work-authorization check*). If that heading has moved after a system update, apply them wherever work authorization is scored:

- Role in Canada → **Not needed**, no penalty.
- Role in the US, JD silent on sponsorship → **Unstated**, neutral — but flag which of TN/L-1A/O-1A plausibly applies given the job title and whether the company has a Canadian entity (an L-1A signal).
- Role in the US, JD offers sponsorship → **Sponsors**, and name the route (H-1B lottery vs TN-friendly vs O-1A-plausible). H-1B alone does not solve the permanent-residency backlog.
- Role in the US, JD states no sponsorship → still a hard blocker **unless** the title is a TN-eligible category (Engineer / Computer Systems Analyst). TN involves no employer petition, so a "no sponsorship" line does not automatically block a TN-eligible role. Note the distinction rather than auto-rejecting.
- Role requiring US person / ITAR / clearance → **hard DQ**, score 1.0 (see above).
- Remote-from-Canada for a US company → **Not needed**; never penalize for being outside the company's home country.

**Remote-dimension scoring:** hybrid outside Canada scores **3.0**, not 1.0. Reserve 1.0 for "on-site 4-5 days/week, no exceptions" in a location with no entry path.

**Targeting priority:**
(a) Canadian roles matching the target archetypes — no visa friction;
(b) US roles at companies with a **known Canadian entity or Canada+US presence** — the L-1A signal, and the highest-value category;
(c) US roles whose title plausibly qualifies as TN "Engineer"/"Computer Systems Analyst";
(d) US roles with O-1A-friendly language ("recognized expert", "thought leadership") or explicit sponsorship.

Always name which pathway a match plausibly supports. Never treat "US role" as a single category.
